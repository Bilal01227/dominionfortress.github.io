How an engagement works

  1. Understand the problem. Start with the business outcome, technical concern or workflow that needs attention.
  2. Define the scope. Confirm requirements, access, authorization, deliverables, exclusions, timeline and fee before work begins.
  3. Deliver and explain. Complete the agreed work, document important findings or implementation decisions, and hand over the result.
  4. Support the next step. Where agreed, help with remediation, integration, follow-up testing or further engineering.
