How an engagement works

  1. Define the outcome. Explain the business or technical problem and what a useful result would look like.
  2. Confirm the scope. Agree systems, access, responsibilities, assumptions, exclusions, timeline and commercial terms.
  3. Execute and communicate. Complete the agreed work, validate important outputs and raise material issues clearly.
  4. Handover and next steps. Provide the agreed documentation, explain decisions and identify optional follow-up work.

Frequently asked questions

Do you test systems without permission?

No. Testing begins only after the owner or authorized representative approves the scope and rules of engagement.

Will the assessment find every vulnerability?

No assessment can guarantee that. Coverage, time, access and system complexity affect what can be found; limitations are documented.

Can you help fix findings?

Yes, remediation engineering or retesting can be separately scoped after the assessment.
