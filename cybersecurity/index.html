How an engagement works

  1. Define the business objective and authorized scope.
  2. Collect evidence using agreed assessment methods.
  3. Validate findings and explain business impact.
  4. Deliver prioritized remediation guidance.
  5. Support remediation or follow-up testing where requested.
Authorization first: All cybersecurity testing requires appropriate client authorization and defined scope. Public scanning does not authorize intrusive testing.
