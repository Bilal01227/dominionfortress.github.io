# Dominion Fortress SEO Growth Playbook
Updated: 2026-11-18
Status: internal working plan; keyword ideas are hypotheses until validated against Search Console and a keyword dataset.

## Operating principles
- Optimize for qualified demand and useful outcomes, not raw keyword counts.
- One dominant search intent per landing page. Do not publish near-duplicate city pages or swap country names into boilerplate.
- Treat Google Search Console as the source of truth for impressions, clicks, CTR and average position. Third-party volume and difficulty are estimates.
- No ranking, indexing, click or impression guarantees. No fabricated clients, locations, certifications, case studies, reviews or outcomes.
- Prioritize unique expertise, clear scope, evidence, practical deliverables and conversion paths.
- Do not use keyword meta tags as a ranking tactic. Keep titles, H1s, visible copy and structured data aligned.

## High-leverage experiments (not loopholes)
1. **GSC opportunity mining:** Export query x page x country x device for 16 months. Identify queries with impressions and average position 4-20. Improve the page already receiving impressions before creating another URL.
2. **CTR experiments:** For pages with meaningful impressions and weak CTR, test one title/description change at a time. Keep a dated change log and compare equivalent periods. Do not promise that a meta description is always used.
3. **Cannibalization review:** Group queries by normalized intent and compare landing pages. If two URLs compete for the same intent, consolidate or differentiate them based on actual user need; do not blindly redirect.
4. **SERP gap review:** For each priority query, inspect the actual top results in target markets. Record missing subtopics, proof, format, freshness and intent. Build a better answer, not a longer imitation.
5. **Tool-to-service funnel:** Every free tool should explain limitations, give a useful result, link to the relevant guide and offer the matching service only where the result warrants it. Track tool completion and qualified contact clicks.
6. **Proof assets:** Publish sanitized, permission-cleared examples: before/after configuration, reproducible methodology, anonymized finding structure, benchmark definitions and implementation notes. Never imply a client case study without permission and evidence.
7. **Topic clusters:** Link each commercial page to two or three relevant guides/tools and link each guide back to the service page. Use descriptive anchors and avoid sitewide exact-match anchor repetition.
8. **International SEO by demand:** Start with countries visible in GSC. Create localized pages only when service availability, language, proof, local requirements and genuinely different search intent justify them. No fake offices or templated city doorway pages.
9. **Conversion instrumentation:** Measure project-form starts/completions, qualified email clicks, WhatsApp clicks and tool-to-enquiry journeys. A traffic increase without qualified leads is not the business objective.
10. **AI search visibility:** Keep pages crawlable, well-linked, factual, attributable and useful. Google's current guidance says ordinary SEO fundamentals apply; there is no special AI file or schema hack that guarantees AI Overviews inclusion.

## Reporting standard
For every report, state:
- Property and search type (Web, Image, Video, News).
- Date range and comparison range.
- Query, page, country and device dimensions.
- Clicks, impressions, CTR and average position.
- Branded vs non-branded classification.
- Export row count and API pagination status.
- Known privacy/anonymization limits: Search Console does not expose every query.
- Changes shipped and their dates.
- Conversions and lead quality, not just ranking metrics.

## 30-day operating cadence
- Days 1-3: export GSC data, audit the highest-impression URLs, validate sitemap and representative canonicals.
- Days 4-7: repair critical crawl/index issues and improve titles/intro sections only where data supports it.
- Week 2: improve the top commercial pages with scope, process, deliverables, fit, limitations and FAQs based on genuine service capability.
- Week 3: publish one genuinely useful guide or tool improvement per priority cluster; add contextual internal links.
- Week 4: inspect query/page changes, conversion events, indexing and technical regressions. Decide the next sprint from evidence.
