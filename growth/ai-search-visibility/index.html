Can you guarantee Google rankings or AI citations?

No. Search systems control crawling, ranking and answer selection. We focus on sound technical foundations, useful content and measurable improvements.

Do you create pages for every keyword?

No. Similar searches should be served by a strong page when intent overlaps. New pages are created only when they satisfy a distinct user need.

Can you work from Search Console data?

Yes, when access or exports are available. Query, page, country and device data help prioritize opportunities and measure changes.
