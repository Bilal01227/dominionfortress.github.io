Email Security Checker

Check public SPF, DMARC and MX signals for a domain. This helps identify common email-domain configuration gaps; it is not an email penetration test.

Why SPF and DMARC matter

SPF and DMARC are public DNS signals used in email authentication. A missing or weak policy can make domain email governance harder and increase spoofing risk. The scanner reports what is publicly observable.
