AI Search Readiness Checker

A practical AEO/GEO-style website audit. It checks whether public pages expose clear titles, headings, useful content, structured data and internal connections that make the site easier to understand.

What this checks

AI Overviews and AI Mode still rely on web content that can be crawled and understood. This tool checks concrete website signals rather than claiming a secret ranking formula.

  • Page titles and descriptions
  • H1 structure and content depth
  • Internal links
  • Structured data presence
  • Public robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt locations
