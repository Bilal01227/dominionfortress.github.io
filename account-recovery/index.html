What we can help with

Different access problems need different recovery paths.

Compromised / hacked account

Contain further access, secure the linked email and phone, document suspicious activity, identify official recovery routes and prepare a clear support report.

Disabled / suspended account

Review the platform notice, identify the stated policy issue, organize relevant context and prepare an accurate appeal through official channels.

Locked / inaccessible account

Work through recovery email, phone, trusted-device and identity-verification options that the platform officially supports.

Lost email / phone / 2FA

Map the remaining recovery factors, secure connected accounts and follow the provider's documented process for regaining access.

Impersonation / business access

Prepare evidence of ownership or authorization and navigate the relevant platform's reporting or administrator-recovery process.

Post-recovery security

Review active sessions, reset credentials through the official provider, enable strong multi-factor authentication and improve backup options.

How the process works

A clear, evidence-led case from first contact to next steps.

Describe the issue

Tell us which platform, what happened, when access was lost and which official recovery steps you have already tried. Do not send secrets.

Review recovery options

We assess the situation, identify relevant official channels and explain what ownership evidence or documentation may be needed.

Follow the authorized route

We help organize the case and next steps. The platform makes the final decision and controls account access.

Safety & trust

Your account stays yours. Your credentials stay private.

We will never ask you for

Your password, OTP, authenticator code, backup codes, session cookie, recovery token, private key or remote access to your device. Do not send these over WhatsApp or email.

What we may request

The platform name, username or public profile URL, a description of the problem, relevant error text and non-sensitive proof that you own or are authorized to manage the account.
