The problem

Security teams do not need another pile of disconnected alerts.

Organizations often discover weaknesses through scanners, audits, cloud reviews and application testing. The hard part is deciding what matters, assigning an owner, coordinating a safe fix and proving that the exposure is actually closed.

01 / FRAGMENTED FINDINGS

Signals in too many places

Security findings can be scattered across tools, spreadsheets, tickets, reports and supplier questionnaires.

02 / UNCLEAR PRIORITIES

Severity is not the whole story

Teams need to consider exploitability, exposure, asset importance, business impact and confidence—not just a scanner score.

03 / UNPROVEN CLOSURE

Closed tickets are not proof

A remediation task should not be treated as complete until the change is reviewed and the relevant weakness is rechecked.

Product workflow

One controlled loop: discover, decide, remediate, verify.

The product direction is designed around the full lifecycle, with AI assisting analysis and coordination while people retain control over consequential security changes.

Core capability areas

Built around decisions and outcomes, not dashboard decoration.

CAPABILITY 01

Vulnerability intelligence

Consolidate findings from authorized assessments and supported integrations, preserve source evidence, and track affected assets.

CAPABILITY 02

AI-assisted validation

Help analysts summarize findings, compare evidence, flag likely duplicates and identify uncertainty for human review.

CAPABILITY 03

Contextual risk prioritization

Combine technical severity with exposure, asset criticality, exploit context and business impact to support prioritization.

CAPABILITY 04

Remediation orchestration

Create accountable remediation tasks, map suggested actions to owners and track status, approvals and exceptions.

CAPABILITY 05

Controlled patching direction

Explore policy-gated patch workflows with pre-checks, explicit authorization, maintenance windows, backups or rollback planning and post-change validation. Autonomous production patching is not claimed as an available feature.

CAPABILITY 06

Verification and evidence

Record retest outcomes, residual risk, remediation evidence and the reason a finding is considered resolved or accepted.

CAPABILITY 07

Supplier security assurance

Organize third-party security questionnaires, control evidence, review dates, ownership and outstanding actions.

CAPABILITY 08

Executive reporting

Translate technical activity into readable risk trends, open remediation, overdue actions and evidence-backed status summaries.

CAPABILITY 09

Recovery and resilience records

Track incident follow-up, recovery-test evidence, lessons learned and corrective actions alongside the security posture record.

AI with guardrails

Automation should reduce workload without creating a new source of risk.

Where AI can assist

  • Summarize technical findings in plain language
  • Correlate duplicate or related findings
  • Recommend remediation options with supporting context
  • Draft change plans, tickets and stakeholder reports
  • Highlight missing evidence and overdue verification

Controls required before action

  • Explicit customer authorization and scoped assets
  • Human approval for material production changes
  • Compatibility and dependency checks
  • Backups, staged rollout and rollback plans where applicable
  • Post-change validation, audit trail and exception handling
Our principle: AI may recommend and coordinate. A production change must follow the customer's access policy, approval requirements and operational safeguards. No claim of safe universal auto-patching is made.

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers for technical and commercial teams.

Is DF Assurance available to buy today?

No. It is a prototype and product-validation effort. It is not presented as a generally available commercial SaaS platform.

Will it automatically patch every vulnerability?

No such capability is claimed. The product direction includes controlled remediation orchestration and policy-gated patch workflows. Any production change would require authorization, compatibility checks, appropriate safeguards and verification.

Does DF Assurance replace penetration testing or vulnerability scanners?

No. The intended role is to help organize findings and manage the remediation lifecycle. Testing and discovery can come from authorized internal teams, existing tools or scoped security engagements.

Can it assess suppliers and compliance evidence?

Supplier-security records and evidence management are part of the proposed direction. Specific frameworks, integrations and compliance coverage must be validated before being promised.

How can an organization participate in validation?

Share your environment, current workflow and biggest remediation bottleneck through the project brief. Any design-partner or pilot engagement would be scoped and agreed in writing.
