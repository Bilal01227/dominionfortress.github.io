How engagements work

Defined scope. Approved access. Documented outputs.

1. Define the problem

Identify the information bottleneck, operational workflow or digital risk the team needs to address.

2. Agree boundaries

Confirm systems, source data, access, deliverables, responsibilities and review requirements before work begins.

3. Deliver and document

Complete the agreed work, document assumptions and provide practical handover notes and next steps.

Scope boundaries: Dominion Fortress provides digital and technology support. It does not provide geological conclusions, mineral-resource or reserve estimates, mining engineering sign-off, investment advice, securities promotion, audit opinions, tax opinions or regulated financial assurance. AI-generated outputs and research require review by the client's qualified personnel. Intrusive security testing requires written authorization.
