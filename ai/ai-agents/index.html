Frequently asked questions

Will an agent be fully autonomous?

Not by default. Autonomy is chosen according to the task's risk. Sensitive actions can require human approval or remain outside the agent's permissions.

Can it connect to existing systems?

Yes, where the required APIs, access rights and technical conditions are available and authorized.

Can you build a document assistant?

Yes. A knowledge assistant can use retrieval over approved documents, with access controls and clear limits on what it can answer.
