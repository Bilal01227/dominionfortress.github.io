Frequently asked questions

Does every workflow need AI?

No. Rules and conventional automation are often more reliable for predictable steps. AI is used where interpretation or language understanding adds value.

Can approvals stay with our staff?

Yes. Approval gates, exception queues and escalation paths can be built into the workflow.

How do you measure success?

We agree practical measures before implementation, such as processing time, manual steps, error rates or completion visibility, where those measures can be observed.
