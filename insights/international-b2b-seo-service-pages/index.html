International B2B SEO: Build Service Pages for Buyers, Not Just Countries

Published 24 November 2026 · Dominion Fortress

International SEO is not solved by cloning one page for every country. B2B buyers need to understand the service, scope, delivery model, evidence and next step. Country targeting should reflect real differences in demand and the offer—not create doorway pages.

1. Choose markets based on delivery fit and buyer demand

Prioritise markets where the business can serve customers reliably, communicate during workable hours, meet contractual requirements and support the expected delivery model. Search volume alone is not a market-entry strategy.

2. Map each commercial page to a distinct buying problem

A penetration-testing page, API-security page and cloud-security page should answer different buyer questions. Explain the intended customer, scope, prerequisites, process, deliverables, exclusions and how to request a quote. Avoid sibling pages that repeat the same generic paragraphs.

3. Localize only when the offer materially differs

A country page is justified when there are real differences in service availability, regulatory context, currency, support hours, delivery terms, local proof or buyer requirements. Otherwise use one strong international service page rather than near-identical geographic variants.

4. Build evidence without manufacturing credibility

Use approved case studies, anonymized deliverable examples, technical methodology, product demonstrations and verifiable credentials. Do not invent testimonials, client logos, certifications, local offices or performance guarantees. Explain what is illustrative and what is actual client work.

5. Connect education to commercial intent

Publish original guides that help buyers make a decision—such as scope checklists, risk comparisons and implementation plans. Link each guide to the relevant service page and a clear enquiry route. Avoid publishing generic articles solely to increase URL count.

6. Measure qualified demand by market

Separate branded from non-branded queries. Track impressions, clicks and landing pages by country, then measure qualified enquiries, sales conversations and won revenue in the CRM. A country with impressions but no relevant leads should not be treated as a successful market.

7. Make the next step easy and specific

Use service-specific enquiry forms, clear response expectations and a concise list of information needed to scope work. Preserve source and service attribution where consent allows. Do not treat a generic click or page view as a lead.
