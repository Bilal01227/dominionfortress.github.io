Hacked or Disabled Account? An Owner's Recovery Checklist

Published 24 November 2026 · Dominion Fortress

A compromised account and a platform-disabled account are different problems. The recovery path depends on what happened, what access remains and which official support process the platform provides. No third party can guarantee reinstatement.

1. Secure the email account first

If the email address or phone number linked to the account is compromised, an attacker may regain access after a password reset. Secure the email provider, review recovery addresses and active sessions, enable multi-factor authentication and check for forwarding rules or unknown app access.

2. Identify the account state accurately

Record whether the account is hacked, locked, disabled, suspended, impersonated or inaccessible because of a lost device. Save the exact platform notice and date. Avoid repeated guesses: too many login or appeal attempts can trigger additional security checks.

3. Preserve evidence without sharing secrets

Keep screenshots of platform notices, profile URLs, timestamps, suspicious messages, transaction records where relevant and proof of ownership. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, backup codes, session cookies or identity documents to an unverified helper.

4. Use the platform's official recovery route

Start from the platform's official help centre or in-product recovery flow. Follow the account-specific steps and provide only the information requested through the verified platform channel. A legitimate helper can organize the evidence and explain the process, but cannot bypass platform decisions.

5. Watch for recovery scams

Treat promises of guaranteed recovery, insider access, paid verification bypasses or requests for remote access as warning signs. Do not pay someone to impersonate platform staff or ask for authentication codes.

6. Harden the account after access returns

Change credentials from a clean device, revoke unknown sessions and connected apps, rotate backup codes, enable phishing-resistant MFA where available and review administrator roles. For business profiles, check ad accounts, payment methods, page ownership and delegated access.
