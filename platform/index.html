How work moves

  1. Discover: understand the business or technical problem.
  2. Assess: collect evidence and diagnostics.
  3. Design: select the appropriate discipline or combination.
  4. Build / Secure: implement the agreed solution.
  5. Operate: maintain, measure and improve.

How to choose a delivery path

The platform is a way to connect capabilities when a business problem crosses disciplines—not a requirement to buy every service. Start with the outcome and use the smallest combination of workstreams that can deliver it.
