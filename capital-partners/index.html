Company overview · Strategic partnerships

Cybersecurity and technology built around operational resilience.

Dominion Fortress is an early-stage, founder-led technology venture focused on authorized cybersecurity assessments, software engineering and AI-enabled workflow automation. We are exploring strategic joint ventures, commercial partnerships and aligned capital relationships.

Current business

Services-led delivery

Current capabilities include authorized security assessments, application and API security work, software development, cloud/IT engineering and AI workflow automation. Engagements are scoped around customer requirements and documented deliverables.

Product direction

DF Assurance

A proposed product direction combining security findings, remediation tracking, recovery-test evidence and supplier security documentation. This concept is under validation; it is not represented as a commercially launched SaaS platform.

Operating model

Founder-led and remote-first

The venture is currently founder-led and operates from Pakistan with an international customer ambition. Delivery capacity, legal structure and geographic expansion would be developed in stages with the right partner.

Investment thesis

Build a repeatable assurance workflow from real customer delivery.

Resilience

Help organizations identify material security weaknesses, prioritize remediation and record whether corrective actions have been verified.

Trust

Help customers organize control evidence and respond to supplier-security requirements using current, reviewable records.

Technology

Turn repeatable service workflows into software only where customer validation demonstrates a recurring need and willingness to pay.

Potential partnership paths

What we are open to exploring.

Commercial / channel partnership

Capital deployment priorities

Use of capital would be milestone-controlled.

01 · Legal and ownership

Establish an appropriate entity, clarify founder ownership, document IP assignment, contracts, accounting and compliance requirements.

02 · Product and security

Build a narrow DF Assurance MVP, secure the platform, implement tenant separation and validate workflows with pilot users.

03 · Paid validation

Deliver paid customer pilots, measure retention and delivery economics, and prove which workflows justify recurring subscriptions.

04 · Go-to-market

Develop focused industry channels, customer acquisition, qualified delivery partnerships and operating capacity.

Stage and diligence disclosure.
Dominion Fortress is an early-stage, unincorporated venture. Its current model is services-led; recurring revenue is not established. Historical project work must not be represented as ARR. DF Assurance is a proposed product direction under validation, not a launched commercial SaaS product. Any investment or joint venture would be conditional on appropriate legal formation, ownership and intellectual-property documentation, financial verification, due diligence and definitive agreements.
